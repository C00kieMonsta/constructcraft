# Architecture: Site location, sun studies and rendering

Part of the architecture notes indexed in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md). Keep each topic in its bullet here; AGENTS.md only lists the topics.

- **Site location** — `cc_core::site`: `ProjectInfo::site` (`Option<SiteLocation>`: latitude, longitude of the project origin in WGS 84 degrees, `elevation` above sea level, `true_north` degrees counter-clockwise from project +Y, `place`, `SiteAddress` parts; repair drops a location off Earth) and the conversion to project meters (`east_north` on the plane tangent at the site, `to_project`/`from_project` with true north; `to_dms`/`from_dms` for IFC). `cc_engine::osm` is pure: `read` turns an Overpass JSON answer (`out geom` or nodes) into a `CadDrawing` in meters (layers `Layer::ALL`, street names along the longest stretch, lines clipped to the square, multipolygon outer ways joined) and `Building` footprints with heights (`height`, `building:levels`, else `Options::default_levels`), `overpass_query` builds the query for the square, `read_places`/`read_reverse`/`read_elevation` read Nominatim and elevation answers; bounded. Commands in `cc_engine::commands::site`: `site_get`, `site_set` (patch; address parts rewrite `ProjectInfo::address`), `site_context_import` (one undo step: a pinned `CadImport` at the origin plus a mass per building of the "Context" mass type, no mass floors). IFC export writes `IfcSite` `RefLatitude`/`RefLongitude` (compound angles), `RefElevation`, `LongName`, `SiteAddress` (town, region, postcode, country) and true north in the model context; import reads them back. In the app, `site_ui.rs` (*Manage → Project → Site Location*): an in-house slippy map (OSM tiles over two `ureq` threads, in memory), Nominatim search and reverse (one request per second, on request only), Open-Meteo elevation, the marker fixed at the center and the square turned with true north; network never in tests.
- **Sun studies** — `cc_core::sun`: `View::sun` (`Option<SunSettings>`, only on views that `ViewKind::takes_sun`: plans, ceiling plans, 3D views, elevations, sections; serde default, no format bump) holds a `SunMode` (`Still` date and time, `SingleDay`/`MultiDay` studies of `SunFrame`s every `SunInterval`, sunrise to sunset or not, bounded by `MAX_FRAMES`/`MAX_DAYS`; `Lighting` azimuth and altitude, `relative_to_view`), the `frame` shown, `show_path`, `shadows` and the `ground` level; `Project::sun_presets` (`SunPreset`, tracked as settings, `EditCtx::sun_presets_mut`) are named modes. The solar position is the low-precision public formulas (`solar_position`, `daylight` with refraction at `HORIZON_ALTITUDE`, `day_path`), deterministic; `Project::sun_site` reads the site (`SiteLocation::utc_offset`, else the longitude's zone, `offset_hours`; the equator at Greenwich without a site) and `SunSettings::state`/`Project::sun_state` give a `SunState` (azimuth, altitude, unit `direction` in project coordinates turned by true north, the frame, `above_horizon`), a view-relative light measured from `view_forward` (`camera_forward` for a live camera). Repair keeps suns on views that take them, values in range (`SunMode::repair`, `repair_presets`) and the ground on an existing level; `EditCtx::finish` → `sync_sun_ground` drops a deleted ground level. `cc_views::sun_path`: `diagram` builds a `SunDiagram` in world coordinates (compass ring turned to true north on the ground level, ticks, axes, the day's path on the dome with its hours, a multi-day study's first and last days, sunrise, sunset, date and the sun; bounded by `MAX_POINTS`, finite), `draw` lays it over plans and projected views as the `Drawing2D::overlay` (no element, not picked, snapped or cropped, but fitted; horizon labels past the ticks, `label_anchor`, the date stacked beyond the sunrise time, `stacked_on`), and `Look::light` lights shaded and textured projected views from the sun (ambient only below the horizon). The sun's shadows in 2D views (`SunSettings::shadows`, in the styles `VisualStyle::colored`: plans, elevations, sections and the 2D form of 3D views; never hidden line or wireframe) are exact polygons, `cc_views::shadow`: `Look::shadows` (`Shadows`: the `ground` height, `Project::sun_ground_height`, the rule the 3D view and the path tracer share: the sun's ground level, else the lowest level, else the model's bottom; and a `Skip` of the elements the view's `Resolver` hides, which cast and catch none, or makes see-through, which cast none, `shadow_skip` in `generate_temporary`) makes `projected::render` build a `Scene` of casters: every opaque face not edge-on to the sun, a closed convex solid (`convex`) as one unit that casts the hull of its points, of the model beyond the frame's near plane closed by the cut's caps (`caps`), in a grid over the units seen from the sun. Which faces catch them is the view's `shadow::Catch`, as drawings are read. `All` (elevations, the 2D form of 3D views): everything shown casts on everything shown. `Site(&Indoors)` (floor plans, set by `plan::style_underlay`) reads the site: the whole model the view shows casts, what stands above the cut plane too, on every face seen from above and on the ground as the sun does (under an eave, a porch or a pergola too), but for what is indoors. `Outdoors(&Indoors)` (sections): the model in front of the section's line casts nothing, and faces indoors catch nothing. `Indoors::of` (made in `generate_temporary` for both) finds storey by storey (from a level to the next; the walls standing `STOREY_PROBE` above the level) the spaces the walls' outlines close (`cc_geom::RoomArrangement::spaces`: rooms whether or not a room element was placed, and the walls' own bodies) that slabs cover by more than `INDOORS_COVER`, united per storey; what covers is a floor, roof or ceiling (`shadow::covers`, by solid category; beams, such as a pergola's, and foundations do not), cast straight down by a second `Scene` (`Scene::covers`). A point is indoors when it is in a space of the storey at its height (`Indoors::holds`) and a slab of another element lies above it (`Scene::above`), so the top of a roof never is. A section takes a face whole, by the point `FACE_PROBE` in front of it (`point_within`): the rooms cut show their plain shading and a wall under an eave, which looks onto the open, keeps the eave's shadow. A floor plan takes the part of each face that is indoors (`Scene::indoors_on`: the slabs over the face within the spaces of the storey `PLAN_PROBE` above it; a face wholly in a space, `encloses`, and wholly covered is skipped) and of the ground out of their shadows (`region::covered_except`). Ceiling plans draw none (`Look::casts_shadows`). For each drawn opaque face turned to the sun, `Scene::on_face` slides the casters standing in front of its plane (`LIFT`) along the sun's rays onto that plane (`cast`: cut to the face, or to its box when it is not convex; one that covers the face ends the search, those inside a convex one are left out) and unites them (`rings`; more than twice `UNION_GROUP` of them a group of neighbors at a time, then the groups' unions together, which costs less), and `Scene::on_ground` does the same on the ground plane when the view looks down (no farther than a sun 5° up reaches). The union is `cc_geom::region::covered` (`covered_except` leaves out what other polygons cover): the polygons' edges welded (`weld`: points within `TOL` one point, edges split where they cross and at points on them; more rounds only when the triangulation still meets a crossing), a constrained Delaunay triangulation of them (spade, in `catch_unwind`), each triangle in or out by its centroid (a triangle of no height with the region on both sides of its line, across a row of such triangles, is a gap of rounding and closed; with the region on one side only, points in a line along a side, it is not), the outline between them chained into rings with their holes keyholed (`pieces`; a hole inside no outline is a failure, never a region without the hole); bounded by `MAX_EDGES` and `MAX_WELD_WORK`. The rings are drawn as black veils that belong to no element (not picked, not overridden; DXF leaves them out), the ground's first and a face's right after its fill and under its lines; `shadow::veil` gives the opacity that leaves `VisualStyle::SHADED_SHADOW` of the lit grey (the textures and consistent colors styles darken alike; fading out edge-on, as the 3D view), `ground_veil` the 3D ground's. Bounded by `MAX_CASTERS`, `MAX_LOOKS`, `MAX_UNION_POINTS` and `MAX_POINTS`: past one the view draws no shadows at all. `cc_render::Light` (`direction`, `shadows`, `ground` height) through `Renderer3D::set_light` (`None`: the camera's light; frames and the shadow map are redrawn only when it changes): `shadow.rs` makes the sun's pipelines on first use and the 2048² shadow map only when a light first casts shadows, `sun.rs` fits the orthographic light view to the scene and the ground quad that catches shadows. `SunMode::conventional()` is the façade drawing's sun (`Lighting` relative to the view at `CONVENTIONAL_AZIMUTH` 225° and `conventional_altitude()` atan(1/√2): rays along a cube's diagonal from over the viewer's left shoulder, so a shadow falls down and to the right as far as its caster stands out; `is_conventional`), and `SunSettings::default_for` gives elevations and sections that sun with shadows when they first get one (the app's Sun Settings dialog). Commands in `cc_engine::commands::sun` (`view_set`/`view_get` `sun` through `SunArg`, `"mode": "conventional"` resetting to that sun, `derived.conventional`, `sun_position`, `sun_frames`, `sun_preset_save`, `sun_presets_list`, `sun_preset_rename`, `sun_preset_delete`; `site_set` `utc_offset`). The app: `sun_ui.rs` (the Sun Settings dialog, *View → Graphics → Sun Settings* and the *Sun* row of view properties; OK/Cancel through `graphics_dialog::Edits`, a saved document left unchanged stays saved) and `sun_overlay.rs` (`SunView`, cached per revision, and for a view-relative sun per live camera yaw: the 3D view's `Light`, its ground the rendered image's `cc_raytracing::environment::ground_height` (the sun's ground level, else the lowest level), and the diagram painted over the image through `modify3d::Frame::segment`, clipped at the near plane); the view cube's compass turns to true north.
- **Rendering** — `cc_core::render_settings`: `View::render` (`Option<RenderSettings>`, only on views that `ViewKind::takes_render`; serde default, no format bump) holds `samples`, `max_depth`, `width`, `height`, `fov_deg`, `exposure` (stops), `tone_map` (`ToneMap::Filmic`/`Standard`), the firefly `clamp`, `clay` and a `RenderEnvironment` (`sky_strength`, `sun_strength`, `ground_color`, `ground_plane`), bounded by the module's `MIN_*`/`MAX_*`/`DEFAULT_*` constants and `MAX_PIXELS`; `is_valid`/`repair`, and repair on load drops settings from views that do not take them and brings values into range. `cc_raytracing` path traces on the CPU through `sort-raytracing`: `RenderScene::build(project, geom, view, camera, settings, temporary)` takes the view's geometry and leaves out what the view's graphics resolver hides (visibility, phase, options, temporary hiding), applies its color, transparency and halftone overrides, its detail level, painted faces and material textures laid as the textures style lays them, with smooth normals across curved facets; `space` turns Z up into the tracer's Y up (`(x, y, z)` → `(x, z, -y)`) about the model's center for `f32` precision; `materials` maps a `Finish` to roughness and shine, metals to metallic, glass (class `Glass` or at least `GLASS_TRANSPARENCY` transparent) to thin glass the sun passes through, other transparency to coverage, textures decoded once (`Textures`, `MAX_TEXTURES`, `MAX_TEXTURE_SIDE`), and `clay` every opaque surface to `CLAY_COLOR` matte (glass kept); `environment` generates the sky map (`sky_map`, `ENV_WIDTH`×`ENV_HEIGHT`), the ground and a distant sun from `Project::sun_state` (`sun_state`; a view without a sun gets `default_sun`, `DEFAULT_SUN_ALTITUDE` high at `DEFAULT_SUN_AZIMUTH` from the viewer), the ground plane at `ground_height` (the sun's ground level, else the lowest level, else the model's bottom); `camera::eye_and_forward` and `camera::fit` (a never moved camera fitted to the picture's field of view and aspect); `run` refines a few samples at a time with a callback returning `Control` (stop between steps) and an optional `Region`, `render_image` renders to the end, and `Image` holds linear radiance (`to_rgba8` exposes and tone maps, `save` writes PNG, JPEG, EXR or HDR); scenes past `MAX_TRIANGLES` are refused, degenerate triangles dropped, and every call into the library runs in `catch_unwind` (`guarded`), a panic becoming `RenderError::Internal`. `plane_axes` and `Solid::facet_shifts` live in `cc_geom` (shared with `cc_render`). The engine: `view_set`/`view_get` `render` (a merge patch through `views::apply_render`; `null` removes it) and `view_render` (`cc_engine::commands::render`: `view`, `path`, `settings` patched for this render only, `region` `[x, y, width, height]`; the view's camera, fitted when never moved, rendered as printed; returns `path`, `width`, `height`, `samples`, `seconds`, `triangles`; changes nothing). The app: `render_ui.rs` (*View → Graphics → Render*, `UiCommand::Render`, available on views that take render settings): a modal window with the picture and the view's settings, rendering on a render thread and a display thread with a stop flag and a generation per render so late pictures are dropped; settings written through `engine_edit::view` under one merge key (one undo step); exposure and tone map remap the linear image without tracing again; *Region*, *Save Image…*, and *Sun Settings…*, which hides the window and reopens it through `Dialogs::render_resume`.
