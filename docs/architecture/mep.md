# Architecture: MEP and pipe series

Part of the architecture notes indexed in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md). Keep each topic in its bullet here; AGENTS.md only lists the topics.

- **MEP** — `cc_core::mep`: plumbing systems and electrical circuits (`MepSystem`, kind, code, color, board, voltage) live in `Project::systems` (tracked as settings); pipes, conduits and cable trays are `ElementKind::Pipe/Conduit/CableTray(MepSegment)` runs with an elevation at each end and the systems they belong to (a tray's `TypeData::CableTray` holds width, height and `TrayStyle`; trays join only trays); a valve is `ElementKind::Valve(Valve { host, at })`, hosted by a pipe at a distance along it (its `TypeData::Valve` holds the `ValveKind`; diameter and system are its pipe's; repair drops one whose host is not a pipe, `remove_element`, copies and `pad_levels` keep it with its pipe); plumbing fixtures and electrical devices are components whose `ComponentDef::mep` holds fixture units or the device kind and power, connected by `Component::system`; a component's `ComponentDef::connectors` (name, system kind, position and direction expressions, diameter) are where runs join it (`cc_geom::component_connectors`): the network attaches each to a node of a system of its kind, and `mep_connect` starts branches there (components without connectors use their insertion point). Vertical runs in the app (`cc_app::tools::segment_tool`): starting on a run at another elevation (`cc_engine::joined_offset_except`) rises or falls to the elevation set first, a new elevation after the first click and a click on the same point make a stand-alone riser, and a run ending over a run of its network at another elevation gets a vertical run down or up to it; `cc_app::run_section_ui` draws pipes, conduits and trays in sections and elevations in a plane parallel to the view (`Frame::unproject`, at the depth of the run the chain starts on, else a section's cut plane; elevations must start on a run), each run on the level under its lower end. `cc_geom::mep` makes the cylinders, tray channels (`ModelGeometry::segments`, `SegmentGeom::tray`) and valve bodies (`ModelGeometry::valves`) and `cc_geom::mep_net` the networks (`ModelGeometry::mep`: nodes where runs meet, edges, derived fittings, valves as nodes on their pipes, fixtures and devices attached to nodes), `cc_geom::sizing` sizes pipes and circuits from them, `cc_views::mep` draws plumbing and electrical plans (`ViewKind::FloorPlan { mep: Some(..) }`, the architecture faded; other floor plans leave MEP out; trays in electrical plans, valves in plumbing plans) and the device and valve symbols, `cc_views::diagram` the panel diagrams (`ViewKind::Diagram`, headings through the `label` of `generate_labeled`), `cc_engine::commands::mep` holds the commands and the circuit loads (`cable_tray` and `valve` the tray and valve commands; IFC export writes `IfcCableCarrierSegment` `CABLETRAYSEGMENT` and `IfcValve`). Systems come in families (`SystemKind::class`, `SystemClass`: plumbing, circuits, structured cabling `Data`, lightning protection `Lightning`); `commands::mep::check_fits` takes the families a member may join (pipes plumbing, conduits and trays circuits and cabling, lightning conductors lightning protection, devices their `DeviceKind::class`), and `MepSystem::panel` is a circuit's board or a data system's rack (`DeviceKind::is_board`). Lightning protection (`cc_core::lightning`): conductors are `ElementKind::LightningConductor(LightningConductor { run: MepSegment, role })` (role air, down or earth; `TypeData::LightningConductor` holds the diameter and material; they join only each other), air terminals and earth electrodes are device components (`DeviceKind::AirTerminal/EarthElectrode`, built-in definitions in `component::device_library`, made on demand by `commands::devices`); `cc_geom::lightning` finds the roof under a point (`surface_top`, where terminals and conductors land) and checks coverage (`check`: roof samples against each air termination point alone by the rolling sphere over the eaves plane, plus the mesh width on flat parts; `LpsClass` gives the radius and mesh), commands in `commands::lightning`. Structured cabling: data outlets (`MepData::Device::ports` jacks) and racks are device components; each rack feeds a `SystemKind::Data` system created by `rack_place`; `Project::outlet_cable` estimates each outlet's cables (`mep::cable_length`: up to the ceiling, across along the axes, down to the rack, plus slack), shown by `cabling_list`, `element_get` and the `CableLength` schedule field; commands in `commands::cabling`. Plans of the `MepDiscipline::Communications` and `Lightning` disciplines draw them (`cc_views::discipline_of`); IFC export writes conductors as `IfcCableSegment` `CONDUCTORSEGMENT`, terminals and electrodes as user-defined `IfcProtectiveDevice`, data outlets as `IfcOutlet` `DATAOUTLET`, racks as `IfcCommunicationsAppliance`, systems as `IfcDistributionSystem` `LIGHTNINGPROTECTION`/`DATA`.
- **Run tags and fitting labels** — the tags of pipes, conduits and cable trays in services plans are tags like a door's (`TagTarget::Pipe`/`Conduit`/`CableTray`, see *Tags* in `annotation.md`): `cc_views::mep::segment`/`tray` give each run a `TagSpot` (along a run at its middle, `CenterBottom`, the host's readable direction; beside a riser or vertical tray, `LeftBottom`; runs shorter than 15 paper mm untagged) and draw it through `tag::prepare`/`draw`, so `tag_set`/`tag_get`, the palette's *Tag* section, dragging, the rotate grip, leaders and *Hide*/*Reset Tag* work as for other tags. Their default styles (`TagTarget::default_style`, 2 mm text along the run) write what plans always wrote through the run fields of `cc_views::tag::field` (`run_field`): `{Systems} {DiameterMark}  {SlopeMark}` for pipes (`ES Ø100  i = 1,0 %`), `{DiameterMark} {Circuits}` for conduits, `{TraySize} {Circuits}` for trays. The template carries no tag type of these (`TagTarget::IN_TEMPLATE`, so its ids and the macros naming them stay): plans draw the default style until `tag_type_create` (or the Tag Types dialog's default row) makes one; format 35 because older builds cannot read these targets. Fitting labels: `View::fitting_tags` (`view_set`/`view_get` `fitting_tags`, services floor plans only, `View::takes_fitting_tags`; the app's view properties row *Label fittings*) labels each fitting of `cc_views::plan_fittings` (the plan's level and discipline) with `cc_views::fitting_lines`: its catalog pieces' `fitting_name`s (main first), else its kind, nominal angle and `Ø` sizes, a missing one those plus the `MissingReason` text, in red and circled. Fittings are not elements: a label's adjustments live in `View::fitting_placements` keyed by `cc_core::tag::fitting_key` (the sorted ids of the runs meeting there, `"12-15"`; `fitting_runs` parses it), repaired by `repair_view_tags` (dropped when a run is gone, bad offsets and angles reset; also in `sync_views`). A label whose runs change (a run split by a new tee) loses its adjustments. Commands (`commands::tags`): `fitting_tag_set` (`keys`, `offset`/`delta`, `rotation`, `hidden`, `leader`, `reset`; one undo step) and `fitting_tags` (each fitting's key, runs, kind, texts, `missing`, placement and drawn box).
- **Pipe series** — `cc_core::pipe_series`: a `PipeSeries` (id `PipeSeriesId`, `Uid`, unique `code`, name, standard, `PipeMaterial`, `PipeUse`, `PipeJoint`, pressure, `SeriesSource`s) holds its sizes (`PipeSize`: label, outside `de`, wall `e`, inside `di`, meters; `row`) and the fittings sold for it (`CatalogFitting`: `FittingRowKind`, angle, `size`/`size2`, socket and laying lengths `z`, all optional; `key`), bounded by the module's `MAX_*`/`MIN_*` and checked by `check` (errors name the row) and `repair`. `Project::pipe_series` is tracked as settings (`EditCtx::pipe_series_mut`; format 30). `cc_core::pipe_file` reads and writes series files (JSON, millimeters, `FILE_FORMAT` 1, `read_series` bounded and refusing unknown enums and bad rows, `write_series` one row per line); `cc_core::pipe_catalog` holds the 22 built-in series (`assets/pipes/*.json`, research notes in `assets/pipes/notes`; `BUILT_IN`, `all`, `series(code)`, `built_in_of`, `Region`; names translated by the `pipe-series-<code>` keys), copied into a project on first use (`Project::built_in_pipe_series`). `TypeData::Pipe` has `series`, `size` (a row's label) and a free type's `inside`; `Project::pipe_row` gives a type's series and row, `ElementType::inside_diameter` the row's `di`, else `inside`. `EditCtx::finish` → `sync_pipe_series` (first, when settings or a pipe type changed) keeps a series type's `diameter` at its row's `de` in the same transaction and makes a type whose series or size is gone free (keeping the diameter); `repair_pipe_series` bounds series, makes codes and uids unique and drops dangling references. The templates' pipe types are sizes of BR-PVC-SOLD and BR-PVC-ESG-N (metric) or US-PVC-SCH40 and US-PVC-DWV (imperial), and files before format 30 link the template's pipe types by name and diameter (`link_old_template_pipes`). `cc_geom::sizing` measures speeds in the type's inside diameter and sizes a run of a series within it (`series_supply_row`, `series_drain_row`; `PipeSizing::size`); `mep_size_apply` keeps a run in its series, making the missing row's type (`created`). `cc_engine::commands::pipes`: `pipe_catalog`, `pipe_series_list`, `pipe_series_get`, `pipe_series_import` (`code`/`path`/`text`, `on_clash`, `sizes`), `pipe_series_export`, `pipe_series_delete` (`detach`), `pipe_type_create`; `type_set` takes `series` (code, name or id; null frees the type) and refuses a size the series lacks before the edit (`check_size`); `type_get` and `element_get` of a pipe add `derived.inside_diameter`, `wall`, `series_code`, `size`. `cc_geom::fitting_catalog` matches a derived pipe fitting whose branches are all pipes of one series (`match_fitting`, from `MepNetwork::build`; `Fitting::catalog`, `branch_runs`): `FittingMatch` has the series, a `MatchStatus` (matched, composite, missing with a `MissingReason`), the `Piece`s (row index, position, body cylinders) and each branch's `laying` length. Elbows take the nearest catalog angle within `ANGLE_TOL`; tees and wyes (`FittingKind::Wye`: a pipe branch 45° from a straight run, `mep_net::is_wye`) the larger run size and the smallest sold branch size from the actual one up, with reducer chains (`reducer_chain`, at most `MAX_CHAIN`, fewest pieces) on the smaller run end and the branch; couplings and reducers by size. A tee or wye needs a straight run and its branch at the row's angle (a row without one has `usual_angle`: 90° for elbows and tees, 45° for wyes), a cross two straight runs at right angles; anything else is missing with `MissingReason::Angle` (no catalog piece is drawn for it). An elbow's bodies (and the generic sockets of an elbow) start `corner_back` behind its center, closing the outer corner, and MEP solids mark their round facets smooth (`Solid::smooth_curved`), so views draw outlines, not facet lines. A reducer's `z` is the gap between its pipe ends, half on each side unless `z2`; values no source gives come from `rule_z`/`rule_socket`/`RULE_BODY`. `build_mep` draws the pieces' bodies, and `ModelGeometry::pipe_cuts` (`pipe_cut_length`) is each run's length less the laying lengths of its matched fittings. `fitting_name(word, row, series)` names a row (the word from `kind_key` through i18n, or `english_word` in IFC). `mep_fittings` adds name, series, sizes, status, reason and composite pieces (`commands::mep::catalog_json`); `element_get` adds `derived.cut_length`; pipe schedules have `InsideDiameter`, `Series`, `Size` and `CutLength`. IFC export writes `PIPE_SERIES_PSET` on pipe segment types and each catalog piece as an `IfcPipeFitting` typed by one `IfcPipeFittingType` per (series, row) (`pipe_fitting_type`), placed at the piece; missing and uncatalogued fittings keep the generic sockets. A fitting schedule (fittings as a schedule source) is not done yet; `mep_fittings` and its CSV cover the takeoff.
- **Pipe Catalog (app)** — `cc_app::pipe_catalog_dialog`: the Pipe Catalog (`UiCommand::PipeCatalog`, *Manage → Project* and the pipe tool's type list; `Dialogs::pipe_catalog`, `open`/`open_on(Selection)`), a list of project series (`pipe_series_list`) and built-in ones not loaded (`pipe_catalog`) filtered by `Region` and use, the selected one's rows from `pipe_series_get` or `pipe_catalog { series }`; *Load series* and *Create types* run `pipe_series_import` (`code`, `sizes`) for a built-in series and `pipe_type_create` per size for a project one, *Import file…*/*Export…* `pipe_series_import { path, on_clash: rename }`/`pipe_series_export`, *Delete* `pipe_series_delete { detach: true }`; edits share `graphics_dialog::Edits` (Cancel undoes them; opened over Type Properties it undoes back to its own start). `grouped_types` groups the pipe tool's type list (`ribbon::options_bar`) by series, smallest size first, free types last; `report_fittings` (after the pipe tool's `pipe_create`) puts the new pipe's catalog fittings, or a missing one's reason, in the status bar (`fittings_message`). Type Properties of a pipe type (`type_dialog::pipe_rows`): series and size pickers through `type_set` (a new series takes the size nearest the type's diameter), the row read-only, a free type's diameter and `inside`. The *Systems* fittings table names catalog fittings and marks missing ones.
