# Architecture: The app (cc_app)

Part of the architecture notes indexed in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md). Keep each topic in its bullet here; AGENTS.md only lists the topics.

- **cc_app** — eframe/egui app. `cad_ui.rs` holds Import CAD (file dialog and dropped files) and the properties and layers of an imported drawing. `AppState` (state.rs) holds the `Document`, cached geometry/drawings, selection, active `Tool`, dialog flags. `app.rs` lays out the quick access toolbar + ribbon, options bar, left panel (project browser over properties), the egui_dock view tabs (`app::show_views`; `tab_list.rs` draws the open views list in the right margin each tab bar keeps free, issue #371: the bar's views by view type in the browser's order, Close Other Views and Close All Views queued in `TabBar::close` for `sync_tabs`, and the tab of every opened view scrolled into view through `TabBar::reveal` and the tab rects `Tabs::on_tab_button` records; the wheel over a crowded bar scrolls it, as egui_dock does), and the home screen (`home.rs`) when `show_home`. Tools (`tools.rs`) run inside 2D canvases; `jobs.rs` runs opening and imports (`actions::open_path`, IFC, SKP, component loads, `cad_ui` CAD and image imports) on a worker thread over a fresh document or a clone of the open one (`jobs::Base`; `Document` is `Clone`), shows the modal progress dialog, blocks shortcuts and dropped files while `jobs::busy`, and installs the result in `jobs::poll` (tests and startup call `jobs::wait`); `side_panels.rs` docks the Project Browser and Properties (`PanelLayout` in `Preferences::panels`: columns per side from the window's edge inward, each stacking panes; moved by dragging a header onto a side strip or another panel's edge, or from the header's menu; the component environment's panel takes Properties' place); `gestures.rs` reads the scroll and pinch over a view (`gestures::read`: a wheel, reporting lines, zooms; a trackpad, reporting points, pans with two fingers and zooms with a pinch, unless `Preferences::trackpad_pans` is off), used by every canvas, the 3D view, the editors, the graph editor and the site map; `preferences.rs` holds the app preferences (`AppState::prefs`: theme, interface scale and text size, ribbon, tooltips, library/macros/projects folders, autosave, recent count), every eframe storage key with their validated load/save, `sync_look` (applies theme, zoom and font scale each frame) and the Preferences window (Ctrl+,; Cancel restores a snapshot); `plugins_ui.rs` loads the plugins folder into the session at startup (`Preferences::plugins_dir`; `plugins_off` are not loaded and run no code, `plugins_files` get the `files` permission; `AppState::replace_document` keeps the extension) and is the Plugins window (Installed and Available tabs; the index and archives are downloaded with `ureq` in background threads, never in tests) and the ribbon's *Plugin Commands* menu, which opens a command in the palette (`palette::open_command`; the palette lists `Session::commands`); `graph_ui.rs` is the visual programming editor (`AppState::graph`, a panel under the views: an in-house canvas on egui's painter, the node search over `graph_nodes`, an inspector, its own undo; runs go through `graph_run`; its keys take over while the pointer is over it, see `graph_ui::keys`); `macro_ui.rs` is the macro editor (`AppState::macro_editor`, a panel in the graph editor's place, never both): the text with its gutter, `script::check` a moment after typing, and stepping through one `script::Stepper` whose steps follow the text while those that ran are unchanged (`Stepper::set_steps`), its keys taking over while the pointer is over it or its text has focus; *Insert Command…* opens the palette in its insert mode (`palette::open_insert`); `library.rs` is the component library window (the `.ccomp` files of `Preferences::library_folders`, edited in Preferences → File locations, listed by `component_library_list`); `phases_dialog.rs` holds the Phases dialog, the phase rows of view and element properties and the Demolish tool, and `AppState::sync` sets the creation phase to the active view's (`phase_creation_set`); two-letter shortcuts are matched in `app.rs::shortcuts`. `preselect.rs` is Tab cycling of the Select tool's pre-selection in 2D views (issue #406): `Drawing2D::hits_at` (the pick order; `hit_test` is its first) plus `wall_ops::wall_chain` after the first wall make the candidates, the raw input hook takes Tab over a canvas the Select tool armed the frame before (`take_tab`; not while a widget has the focus or a modal is open), `frame` applies the steps and the reset rules (pointer 3 points off the anchor, another view, a new revision, a click), and the status bar shows the candidate through `AppState::hint` (`StatusHint`, set each frame, in place of the message).
