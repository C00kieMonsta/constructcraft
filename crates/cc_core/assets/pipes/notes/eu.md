# EU pipe series: notes

Files were generated by `../../gen_eu.py`. Every source is listed in each file's `sources`. `di` is always
computed as `de - 2e` (one decimal place). None of these catalogues publish an inside diameter.

## EU-PVCU-1452-PN10 / PN16 (EN ISO 1452-2)

- The issue's table matches the FIP (Aliaxis) handbook and the Fasoplast catalogue for every size from d20
  upward. Both sources give the same values: PN10 is S10 up to d90 and S12.5 from d110, and PN16 is S6.3 up
  to d90 and S8 from d110.
- **Difference at d16 PN16.** The issue gives e = 1.2 mm (di 13.6). FIP sells d16 PN16 at **1.5 mm** under
  an SDR 11 product code, and the standard's tables put 1.5 mm in every class at the small sizes. I used
  1.5 mm. di is therefore 13.0 mm, not 13.6 mm.
- PN10 starts at d32 in FIP's range, as in the issue.
- In FIP's EN ISO 1452 range, PN16 changes to SDR 17 at d110. FIP's industrial EN ISO 15493/DIN range and its
  French NF range stay at SDR 13.6 (d110 × 8.1). Georg Fischer's catalogue also lists d110 PN16 at 8.1. Both
  variants are on the market. These files follow the EN ISO 1452 water-supply design (C = 2.0 from d110).
- Fitting data comes from FIP: `socket` is L, `z` is Z and `od` is E. The fittings are rated PN16, and the
  same fittings are listed in the PN10 file.
  - Couplings: z is half of FIP's Z, the gap between pipe ends. These rows are marked `derived`.
  - Reducers: these are reducing bushes. Their spigot goes into a socket of size d.
    - `socket` is the spigot length.
    - `z` is FIP's Z, the distance from the spigot end to the bottom of the small socket.
    - `socket2` is not published. It is derived from the EN ISO 1452-3 minimum socket depth, 0.5·d + 6 mm.
      This rule matches FIP's own socket depths exactly (for example 61 mm at d110 and 86 mm at d160).
  - One double-socket reducer (110×90) is published with all its values.
- FIP gives the d140 90° elbow as Z = 77, while the d140 tee is 72. I kept the elbow value as printed. It may
  be a misprint.
- The 45° wyes (FIP "YIV") were left out. It was not clear which run Z and Z2 refer to.

## EU-PELD-PN4 / PN6 (LDPE irrigation)

- There is no single EU standard for LDPE pipe walls, and the national series differ at small sizes:

  | Source | Basis | PN4 | PN6 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Centraltubi catalogue (**used**) | UNI 7990 PE32 | 16 1.4, 20 1.6, 25 1.7, 32 1.9, 40 2.4, 50 3.0, 63 3.7 | 16 1.6, 20 1.7, 25 2.2, 32 2.8, 40 3.5, 50 4.3, 63 5.4 |
  | AZUD | UNE 53131 PE32 | 25 2.0, 32 2.0, 40 2.4, 50 3.0, 63 3.8 (about SDR 17, 2.0 mm minimum) | 20 2.0, 25 2.3, 32 2.9, 40 3.7, 50 4.6, 63 5.8 (SDR 11) |
  | Elydan (France) | "4 bar" = SDR 13.6, "6 bar" = SDR 9 | 25 2.0, 32 2.4 | 16 2.0, 20 2.3, 25 2.8, 32 3.6, 40 4.5, 50 5.6 |
  | Balitrand (France) | 6 bar | — | 20 × 2.4 |

  I used the Centraltubi UNI 7990 table because it is the only one that covers d16–d63 for both classes.
  That table's PDF text was extracted from columns. I mapped it by its layout: the thinnest column is PN4 and
  the middle one is PN6. The Spanish AZUD walls equal EN 12201-2 PE40 at SDR 17 and SDR 11. That would be a
  defensible alternative if a standards-based series is preferred.
- Fittings are Cepex PP compression fittings, rated PN16 and made for PE pipe to EN 712/ISO 14236.
  - `socket` is null, because the insertion depth is not published.
  - `z` is Cepex Z.
  - `od` is the nut diameter E.
  - Coupling z is half of the published gap. These rows are marked `derived`.
  - Reducers are reducing couplings, and z is the published Z.
- Thin-walled LDPE usually needs insert liners in compression fittings. Barbed insert fittings are also
  common, but I found no catalogue with dimensions for them. The files use joint "compression".

## EU-PVCU-1329 (soil and waste inside buildings, EN 1329-1, application area B)

- Walls are from Fasoplast's "Type B" column: 32–75 at 1.8, 90 at 1.9, 100 at 2.0, 125 at 2.5, 140 at 2.8,
  160 at 3.2 and 200 at 3.9.
  - Fasoplast lists d100 but **no d110**.
  - d110 = 3.2 comes from National Plastic's BS EN 1329-1:2000 area "B" table. 110 × 3.2 is also the common
    market pipe (Wavin indoor 110 × 3.2 shop listings).
  - Some tables are said to give 110 × 2.2 for area B. I could not confirm this from a public source, so it is
    an open question.
- Fasoplast's BD column, for reference: 32–140 at 3.2, 160 at 4.0 and 200 at 4.9.
- d80 is not in the file because no source listed it. National Plastic's 32/40/50 rows are UK sizes
  (36/43/56) and were not used.
- **No public fitting dimensions were found** for continental EN 1329 solvent fittings, so `socket` and `z`
  are null in every row.
  - The rows come from the fitting types that Fasoplast (32–200: curves, branches, reducers) and the
    Ferroplast price list show for the series: elbows 87.5/67.5/45, single branches 45/67.5/87.5 and
    reductions.
  - The sizes per fitting were not confirmed one by one, because the Ferroplast PDF does not extract its
    numbers.
  - The 30° and 15° bends are listed only for d110/125/160. They are an assumption that needs checking.
- I did not use a socket-depth rule for EN 1329. The standard text is paywalled.
- Joint: many continental markets (for example Poland and the Czech Republic) sell EN 1329 PVC-U with ring
  seals. Greece, Spain and Italy commonly use solvent cement. The file says "solvent".

## EU-PVCU-1401-SN4 / SN8 (buried sewer)

- SN4 is SDR 41: 110 at 3.2, 125 at 3.2, 160 at 4.0, 200 at 4.9, 250 at 6.2 and 315 at 7.7.
- SN8 is SDR 34: 110 at 3.2, 160 at 4.7, 200 at 5.9, 250 at 7.3 and 315 at 9.2.
- These values agree across the KG catalogue, Ostendorf and National Plastic.
- No source lists **d125 SN8**. SDR 34 would give 3.7 mm, but it is not included.
- Fasoplast's older Greek "Series 41" (110 at 3.0, 125 at 3.1, 160 at 3.9) is a different, older series and
  was not used.
- Bends:
  - `z` is the KG catalogue's z1 for d110, 200, 250 and 315 (KG "87°" is written as 87.5).
  - **The d160 bend values in that catalogue are inconsistent** (15° = 32, 67° = 96, 87° = 64). Another
    Ostendorf sheet gives 13 for 160/15°. I set every d160 bend z to null.
  - No source gave d125 bends, so they are left out.
- Branches (45° wyes) and reducers are listed with null z. Their z1/L1/L2 meaning in the catalogues was
  unclear.
- Couplings are KG double sockets, with z = half of Z. These rows are marked `derived`.
- `socket` is null for all fittings.
  - Pipe socket lengths are published, but they include the seal zone, so I did not use them.
  - Magnaplast gives t = 76 / 110 / 120 / 140 / 160 for d110 / 160 / 200 / 250 / 315.
  - Ostendorf gives t = 66 / 68 / 84 / 106 / 128 / 162 for d110 / 125 / 160 / 200 / 250 / 315.
- The same KG fittings (normally SN4) are listed in the SN8 file.

## EU-PE100-SDR11 (optional series 5)

- Walls d20–d110 come from Deschacht's EN 12201-2 PN16 variant table (20 × 2.0 … 110 × 10.0). They match
  Uponor from d32.
- Georg Fischer's DIN 8074 industrial pipe uses 20 × 1.9 and 32 × 2.9. That is not EN 12201 and was not used.
- Fittings are the Cepex compression range (d20–d110), interpreted as in the LDPE files.
- Electrofusion fittings were not covered. No public catalogue with z values was found in the time
  available.

## EU-CU-1057 (copper)

- Not done. I found no public source with EN 1254-1 capillary or press fitting dimensions, and the time ran
  out.

## Fixes made when the files were first validated

- EU-PVCU-1452-PN10 and PN16: the d110 × d90 reducing bush (derived socket2, z = 9) and the published
  double-socket reducer (z = 4.5) had the same key (kind, size, size2), which makes matching ambiguous.
  The reducing bush row was removed; the fully published double-socket reducer stays.
