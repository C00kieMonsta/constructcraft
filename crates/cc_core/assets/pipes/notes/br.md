# Brazilian series: notes

The files are generated by `../gen_br/gen.js`, which holds the transcribed catalog values. Every
source is listed in each file's `sources`. Catalog PDFs were read as text with `pdftotext`. Their
tables are often laid out in columns, so each transcription was checked against row and column
sums such as A = 2B + gap. The drawings themselves could not be viewed, because no renderer was
available. So when a published letter (Z, Z¹, Z²) could not be pinned down from the text alone,
the value was left `null` and kept in these notes.

## General mapping of catalog letters

- Krona catalogs (soldável, esgoto, PPR, CPVC): `B` is the socket depth (`socket`), `Z` the laying
  length (`z`), `A` the fitting body's outside diameter (`od`, soldável only), `C` an overall length.
- Tigre Aquatherm (CPVC): `B` is the socket depth. `A` is the center-to-end distance for elbows, the
  end-to-end length for couplings, and the run end-to-end length for tees. `C` is center to branch
  end. z is derived (`derived` in the row).
- CEHOP/SE copper specification: the coupling table gives `A` (end to end) and `B` (socket depth).
  The elbow `A` is center to end, the tee `A` is the run end to end, and the tee `B` is center to
  branch end. Socket depths for the elbows and tees are taken from the coupling table (`derived`).
- Coupling `z`: the catalogs give the gap between the pipe ends (Krona `Z`) or the overall length.
  `z` = gap / 2 as FORMAT.md defines it (`derived`).

## 1. BR-PVC-SOLD (NBR 5648)

- Pipe OD and wall thickness agree across Krona, Tigre and Amanco Wavin (20×1.5 … 110×6.1). `di` is computed.
- Fittings are from Krona. Krona's published elbow 90 `Z` is up to 2 mm larger than `C − B`, for
  example 110: C 124, B 63, Z 63. The published Z was kept anyway.
- Tigre's sheet gives only overall dimensions, with no socket depth, so it was used for cross-checks only.
- Left out:
  - Cruzeta: Tigre lists it, but its table is garbled (only 20 and 50 are readable).
  - Joelho 90° de redução (25×20, 32×25): the format has no reducing elbow.
  - Curva 90/45 (long-radius bends): they would duplicate the elbow rows. Krona values for a later
    variant: curva 90, Z 40.5 to 239 for 20 to 110; curva 45, Z 34 to 154.
  - Bucha de redução curta/longa: spigot × socket bushings, which do not fit the socket-socket
    reducer definition.
- Luva de redução, Krona row 32×25: the D¹/D² columns are printed swapped. Sizes were taken from the label.

## 2./3. BR-PVC-ESG-N / BR-PVC-ESG-R (NBR 5688)

- Joint is set to `ring`. DN 50 and up have dual sockets (ring or solvent). DN 40 ("secundário") is
  solvent only. Socket depth `B` is the deep ring/primary socket. The Krona couplings also have a
  shallower solvent socket: SN 21.4/26/31/49.2, SR 21.4/26/31.5/49.5 mm for DN 50/75/100/150. It is not in the files.
- Pipe OD: 40.0, 50.7, 75.5, 101.6, 150.0. Wall: SN 1.2/1.6/1.7/1.8/2.6, SR 1.8/1.8/2.0/2.5/3.6.
  Krona and Wavin agree. The SR pipe table in Krona has shifted columns, so the Wavin SR values were used.
- Tees, junções (wye) and reducing tees/wyes have `z`/`z2` = null. Krona gives Z¹/Z² (or a single
  Z), but whether each is run or branch could not be established from the text, and simple checks
  (C/2 − B, branch radius) fail. The published values, if someone can check the drawing:
  - SN tê: DN 50 Z¹ 39.8, Z² 28.3; DN 75 55.5, 35.0; DN 100 46.0, 53.0; DN 150 113.0, 53.0. DN 40: Z 22.5.
  - SN tê de redução (Z¹, Z²): 75×50 27.6, 50.8; 100×50 24.5, 63.5; 100×75 41.0, 67.1; 150×100 54.0, 93.2.
  - SN junção simples (Z): DN 40 50.5; 50 73.0; 75 97.6; 100 130.0; 150 190.5.
  - SN junção de redução (Z¹, Z²): 75×50 80.3, 85.3; 100×50 92.5, 103.3; 100×75 110.0, 115.6; 150×100 160.0, 177.0.
  - SR tê (Z1, Z2): DN 50 40, 27; DN 75 44, 40; DN 100 45, 55; DN 150 62, 78. DN 40: Z 21.5.
  - SR tê de redução (Z1, Z2): 75×50 29.5, 68.0; 100×50 30.0, 64.0; 100×75 45.0, 67.5; 150×100 56.0, 98.2.
    The 100×75 row is printed "150x75", but its D1 is 101.6.
  - SR junção simples (Z): DN 40 53; 50 67.5; 75 95; 100 127; 150 186.5.
  - SR junção de redução (Z1, Z2): 75×50 77.2, 82.4; 100×50 92.5, 103.3; 100×75 92.5, 114.0;
    150×100 152.2, 162.3. The 75×50 sockets are printed B1 39 / B2 44, which looks swapped, so they were left null.
- The SN 75×50 reducing junção is printed "70x50" in Krona.
- Redução excêntrica: the large end is a spigot (ponta) and the small end a socket, so `socket`
  (large end) and `z` are null. Only the small-end socket is given.
- Elbows: Krona's elbow 45 Z does not always equal C − B (DN 50: C 56.2, B 39, Z 13). The published Z was kept.
- Curvas (short and long 90/45) were left out because they would duplicate the elbow rows. Krona SN
  curva 90 longa Z: DN 50 89.0, 75 156.7, 100 182.5, 150 426.5. Curva 45 longa Z: 93.8, 142.5, 168.5, 259.0.
- Amanco Wavin esgoto sheets were used only for pipe dimensions. Their fitting tables have
  letters but no drawings in the text.

## 4. BR-CPVC (NBR 15884)

- Conflict in pipe OD. The Tigre Aquatherm product table (p. 12) and Krona give 15×1.6, 22×2.0,
  28×2.5 (Krona: 28.1). The header table on Tigre p. 1 gives 15.9×1.8 and 28.6×2.6, which are
  copper-tube-size values. The p. 12 values were used. 35 and 42 are 34.9 and 41.3 in both Tigre tables.
- The Tigre joelho 45° `A` values for 35/42/54 (64, 75, 98) are not consistent with a
  center-to-end length, so z = null for those sizes.
- Tê 15: the run uses A = 46 and the branch uses C = 22 (z2 = 9). Tê de redução: branch z2 is null
  because the sheet gives no branch dimension.
- Cross-check against Krona (15/22/28): elbow 90 z 9.6/13.1/16.1 vs derived 10/13/16. Tee z
  9.6/13.1/16.1. Coupling gap 3.2 vs derived 3.0.
- Left out: bucha de redução and luva de redução (their tables are garbled), and curva 90 (15/22/28 only, R 38/55/70).

## 5. BR-PPR-PN20 (NBR 15813)

- PN 20 was chosen because it is the general hot and cold building line. Krona sells PN 20 and
  PN 25 pipes, and its fittings are PN 25 for both. PN 25 walls (SDR 6) are 3.4, 4.2, 5.4, 6.7,
  8.3, 10.5, 12.5, 15.0, 18.3 for 20 to 110. PN 20 walls are SDR 7.4 (20×2.8 … 110×15.1).
- Sizes follow the PPR series: 20, 25, 32, 40, 50, 63, 75, 90, 110 (not 60/85).
- The coupling table is headed "Luva de Redução F/F PPR" in the catalog but lists single sizes 20
  to 110. It was read as the plain luva. z = (C − 2B)/2 gives 2.0 to 8.5 mm, and 90 and 110 look large.
- Reducer: the luva de redução has three sizes (25×20, 32×20, 32×25). z = C − B − b, derived.
- Socket depths conflict. Krona B is 14.5/16/18/20.5/23.5/27.5/30/33/37. The Tigre manual
  (tigre_manual_tecnico, "Tabela 1 - Profundidades de inserção") gives 12/13/14.5/16/18/24/26/29
  for 20 to 90. Krona was used.
- No reducing tee table was found. The bucha de redução M/F is a spigot × socket, so it was left out.

## 6. BR-COBRE-E / BR-COBRE-A (NBR 13206)

- Tube dimensions come from the CEHOP/SE public specification (table 01). It also lists class I. The labels
  66/79/104 mm correspond to OD 66.7/79.4/104.8.
- The fittings (bronze/copper capillary solder fittings) are identical in both files.
- Curva 45°: the table gives A = B = socket depth, which makes the meaning of A unclear, so z = null.
- Reducers: only spigot × socket "buchas de redução" are listed, so they were left out.
- This is a state agency specification, not a manufacturer catalog. Manufacturer PDFs (Eluma and
  others) were not found online during this pass.

## 7. PEAD (NBR 15561): not written

- The Tigre PEAD sheet (tigresite …/FT-PEAD_2022-12-1.pdf) returned HTTP 403 and an invalid PDF.
  No other complete public PE 100 SDR 11 table for 20 to 110 was found quickly, so this series was skipped.
